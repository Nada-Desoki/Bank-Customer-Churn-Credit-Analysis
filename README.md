# Bank-Customer-Churn-Credit-Analysis
Bank Customer Churn &amp; Credit Analysis using SQL, Python and Power BI

# Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn behavior and credit card activity within a banking environment. The objective was to identify customer segments with higher churn risk, evaluate customer behavior patterns, assess financial performance, and provide data-driven business recommendations to improve customer retention and engagement.

---

# Tools Used

- SQL Server
- Python (Pandas, NumPy)
- Power BI

---

# Project Workflow

Data Cleaning & Preprocessing (Python)

- Cleaned and prepared the dataset using Pandas and NumPy.
- Handled missing values and data inconsistencies.
- Performed data validation before analysis.

Database Design & Analysis (SQL)

- Applied database normalization techniques.
- Wrote SQL queries to calculate KPIs and perform analytical reporting.
- Extracted customer, behavioral, and financial insights.

Dashboard Development (Power BI)

- Built Power BI dashboards to analyze customer demographics, customer behavior, and financial performance.

---

Dashboard Pages

# Page 1: Customer Demographics & Churn Analysis

Objective

Analyze customer demographics and identify segments with the highest churn rates.

Key Insights

- The overall customer churn rate reached 16%.
- Female customers showed higher churn behavior than male customers.
- Customers aged 40–55 represented the highest churn segment.
- Lower-income, married, and graduate customers showed higher churn concentration.
- No female customers were represented in the 60K+ income category.

# Page 2: Customer Activity & Behavior Analysis

Objective

Understand customer activity patterns and their relationship with churn behavior.

Key Insights

- Customers with higher inactivity months were more likely to churn.
- Frequent service calls appeared to be an early warning indicator of churn.
- Customers experiencing declines in transaction activity showed higher churn rates.
- Customer spending peaked around 36 tenure months before gradually declining afterward.

# Page 3: Financial & Credit Analysis

Objective

Evaluate customer credit behavior and assess financial performance indicators.

Key Insights

- Average credit utilization remained relatively low at 13.32%.
- Existing customers maintained higher revolving balances than churned customers.
- Blue card holders generated the highest total spending volume.
- The bank appears to maintain a relatively stable level of credit risk.

# Business Recommendations

* Develop targeted retention campaigns for high-risk customer groups.

* Introduce personalized offers and loyalty programs targeting female customers to improve engagement and retention.

* Design tailored micro-finance and credit products for female entrepreneurs to safely break the 60K credit limit ceiling, unlocking and activating the bank's stagnant available credit.

* Tailor mid-age segment offers (40–55 years) based on marital status:

- For Married: Introduce "Family Credit Packages" with zero-interest installments for school fees and medical insurance.

- For Non-Married (Single/Divorced): Launch offers focused on financial independence and lifestyle, such as installment plans for retirement savings, travel/leisure discounts, and extended cashback on personal high-value purchases to activate their stagnant credit.


* Improve customer service quality and proactively monitor dissatisfied customers using the 2-call threshold.

* Track inactivity months and declining transaction behavior as early churn indicators.

* Encourage customer engagement through cashback offers, rewards, and personalized promotions.

* Introduce structured Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) or zero-interest installment options directly linked to the available credit to encourage spending among risk-averse customers.

* Introduce loyalty programs and special benefits for long-tenure customers to maintain engagement after the 36-month spending peak.

* Provide premium offers for high-value customers, while allocating 80% of marketing partnerships and cashback rewards to Blue card holders to maximize volume and revenue.

* Use customer behavior and financial indicators to support early churn prediction strategies.

Note:

"The bank is in a highly secure financial position with minimal credit risk (13.32% utilization). Therefore, the bank does not need to raise existing credit limits. Instead, focus must shift entirely toward activating and driving engagement within the massive stagnant available credit already allocated to customers."
